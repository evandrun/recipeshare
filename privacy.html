YOUR INFORMATION, CLEARLY EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

RecipeShare is a community cookbook for finding and sharing recipes. This policy explains what information the RecipeShare website handles and how it is used.

Information we receive

  • Google sign-in: If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your name, email address, and Google account identifier to Supabase Auth. Google may also provide a profile photo. RecipeShare uses your account information to sign you in, show your name, and associate recipes and ratings with your account. RecipeShare does not receive or store your Google password.
  • Recipes and photos: Recipe submissions can include an author name, title, cuisine, category, preparation time, ingredients, steps, and a photo link. Signed-in users may upload a photo. Recipe and photo content is visible to the public.
  • Ratings: Rating values are stored with a recipe. For guest ratings, the browser stores a random identifier in local storage so the same browser can update its rating.
  • Browser storage: Supabase Auth keeps the sign-in session in browser storage so you can remain signed in. You can sign out from the site to end that session in the browser.

What is public

Recipes, author display names, recipe photo links or uploaded photos, and ratings are available to visitors. Your Google email address and account identifier are used for account functions and are not displayed on public recipe pages. A name from your Google profile may appear as the author name on a recipe you share.

How information is used and shared

RecipeShare uses information to provide sign-in, recipe sharing, ratings, photo storage, and account ownership features. Google provides the sign-in service. Supabase provides authentication, database, and photo storage services. Vercel hosts the website. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. RecipeShare does not sell Google account information or use it for advertising.

If you add a photo by URL, your browser loads that image from the website that hosts it. The host's own privacy practices apply to that request. RecipeShare does not control third-party image hosts.

Retention and your choices

Signing out ends the active sign-in session in your browser; it does not remove recipes or ratings you have submitted. You can edit or delete recipes you own while signed in. To request help with your account information or removal of content, contact us at ethanjvd6@gmail.com. You can also review or revoke RecipeShare's Google sign-in access in your Google Account settings.

Security

RecipeShare uses HTTPS and Supabase access policies to protect account-owned actions. No website or storage service can guarantee perfect security. Keep your Google account secure and sign out when using a shared device.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as RecipeShare changes. The current version will appear on this page with its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email ethanjvd6@gmail.com.
